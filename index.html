Booking takes three decisions.

Nothing else is asked until your time is held. Built to be done on a phone in under a minute.

  1. Treatment

    Choose what you need. Each one shows how long it takes and what it costs.

  2. Practitioner

    Pick someone, or take whoever is free first. You'll see when each person is next free.

  3. Time

    The next seven days, with the gaps that are really there. Tap a time and it's held for you.

Then your details, and only then: name, mobile and email, while your time stays held.
